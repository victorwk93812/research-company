1. Apply gates (such as Hadamard + CNOT) and let (e0, 1), (2, 3), (4, e1) get entangled with each other.
2. Apply more gates to let (1, 2), (3, 4) be entangled with each other.
3. Measure 1, 2, 3, and 4 and apply corresponding single qubit gates to e0 and e1, so that they're entangled in a Bell state.
